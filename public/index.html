The candy

What Mexican Fire is

Mexican Fire is a dark chocolate bar folded with sun-ripe prickly pear, sliced jalapeños, and a dusting of chili powder. It is built as a trip, not a snack: first the cocoa, then the fruit, then the heat that lingers like late light on the water.

Bold flavors. Brighter places.

Four notes. One getaway.

We started with rich dark chocolate — deep, glossy, and a little smoky. Then we packed it with the flavors of a Mexican shoreline: cactus fruit that tastes like a tropical orchard, jalapeños for a clean green heat, and chili powder that blooms after the sweetness has already won you over.

It is not a gimmick pepper bar. It is a new experience: sweet enough to crave, warm enough to remember.

The experience

How the getaway unfolds

Mexican Fire is meant to be eaten slowly. Each square is a short trip: cocoa, fruit, fire, and a finish that asks for one more piece.

  1. 01

    The melt

    Dark chocolate coats the palate. Rich, round, a little roasted.

  2. 02

    The fruit

    Prickly pear flashes pink and sweet — like cactus candy in the sun.

  3. 03

    Jalapeño lifts the middle. Green, lively, impossible to ignore.

  4. 04

    The glow

    Chili powder stays behind. You taste the heat. You crave the sweet.

Inspired by Cancún

Flavor with a shoreline

Mexican Fire is associated with the beaches of Cancún: limestone light, turquoise water, hibiscus in the heat, and a sunset that turns the whole coast red. This is candy as a destination — an exotic getaway you can unwrap anywhere.

We built the bar the way a good trip feels. Bright on arrival. Warmer the longer you stay. Hard to leave.
