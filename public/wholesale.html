Why they pay extra

A premium product is an excuse to linger.

People will spend more when the bar feels like a destination. Mexican cacao. Named fruit and chile. A wrapper that looks like a ticket to Cancún. In a Whole Foods or Sprouts set, that reads as a gift, a treat, a small luxury, the item they justify. That is the whole strategy: fewer doors, better shelves, a higher ticket, a story that holds.
